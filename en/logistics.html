Preparing a professional assignment in Senegal

Logistics coordination starts with a clear picture of the team's movements and requirements. The following information helps define what needs to be arranged before the assignment begins.

Dates, locations and participants

Share the start and end dates, cities or sites to visit, known timings and the number of people involved.

Transport and accommodation

Describe expected journeys, location changes, arrival times and accommodation needs so the practical arrangements can be assessed.

Specific requirements

Note travel constraints, schedule limitations or GPS and geolocation needs related to the assignment.
