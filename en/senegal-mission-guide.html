Planning a professional mission in Senegal: a practical checklist

Clear preparation helps coordinate communication, travel and local arrangements. This checklist covers the main details to clarify before your team arrives or a programme begins.

Before travelling

1. Define the schedule and scope

Gather the dates, locations, known timings, number of participants and the purpose of each meeting. Separate confirmed commitments from items still to be checked. If several cities or sites are involved, order the journeys and allow time between appointments.

2. Plan local movements

List the sites to visit, arrival and departure times, transfers and transport requirements. If accommodation coordination is needed, share the dates, preferred area, number of people and known constraints.

3. Prepare working languages

For each meeting, specify the languages, participants and expected format. A bilateral discussion, a working meeting and a conference may call for different interpretation arrangements. If possible, share an agenda, names and specialised terminology in advance.

4. Identify documents to translate

List the documents, source language, target language, approximate volume and target date. Explain who will use the material. If a process requires certified translation or a specific formality, mention it early so applicable requirements can be clarified.

5. Keep coordination in one brief

Bring the schedule, local contacts, language needs, travel details and priorities into one brief. Identify one contact for updates and record schedule changes to avoid conflicting information.

Frequently asked questions

What should you send when requesting support?

Which information is most useful?

Dates, locations, languages, participant numbers, meeting type, documents and required deadline. Items that are still uncertain can be marked as provisional.

Can several services be coordinated?

You can describe language and practical requirements in one brief. The scope, availability and arrangements for each service should then be confirmed.

When should we get in touch?

As early as possible, once dates and key requirements are known. This helps clarify missing details before arrangements are confirmed.

How do we request an estimate?

Send a summary of the programme, place, dates, languages, document volume and logistical needs through our Contact page.
